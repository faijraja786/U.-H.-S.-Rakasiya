ABOUT EDUFUTURE

About Us

EduFuture is an educational learning portal associated with U.H.S. Rakasiya. Its goal is to provide students with a simple digital space for learning materials and practice.

Our Purpose

To support students with regular practice, subject-wise learning, and a better understanding of their progress.

What Is Available on EduFuture?

  • Learning resources for students in Classes 9 and 10.
  • Objective and subjective practice tests.
  • Study Notes and Previous Year Questions (PYQ).
  • Learning features such as quiz results, progress, and leaderboards.
  • Teacher-guided doubt solving and answer evaluation.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide a useful, clear, and student-friendly learning experience. Website content and features may be updated from time to time. EduFuture does not claim to be an official website of any examination board or government institution.
