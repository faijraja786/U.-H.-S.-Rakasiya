Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use EduFuture and how it is used. Please read it carefully before using the website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name, email address, and profile or class information when you create an account or sign in.
  • Quiz attempts, submitted answers, marks, progress, and teacher review information.
  • Doubts or information you submit for website support.
  • Limited log, device, or browser information required for security and technical operation.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate accounts, provide educational features, evaluate quizzes, display results and ranks, enable necessary teacher and administrator controls, maintain security, and improve the service.

3. Firebase and Service Providers

EduFuture login and some data services may rely on technology providers such as Google Firebase. These providers may process data as necessary to operate their services under their applicable policies.

4. Sharing Information

We do not intend to sell personal information. For educational operations, relevant teachers and authorized administrators may view a student's name, class, answers, and results. Information may also be shared when required for legal, security, or service-operation reasons.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Browser storage, cookies, or service-worker cache may be used for login sessions, essential website functions, and the PWA experience. Changing these settings in your browser may affect some features.

6. Children's Privacy

EduFuture is an educational service that may be used by students. Minors should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian and their school. Do not share another person's private information.

7. Security and Data Requests

We try to use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, but complete security of any internet-based system cannot be guaranteed. Keep your account information and password secure. Contact a teacher or authorized school administrator for requests relating to your data.

8. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
