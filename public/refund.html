Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy will apply to any paid digital service or Premium membership offered by EduFuture in the future.

Current Status: Premium payments are not currently active on the EduFuture website. Do not send money directly to anyone claiming to represent the website.

1. Payments and Receipts

When payments are introduced, the price, membership duration, included features, and applicable terms will be clearly displayed before payment. A digital confirmation or receipt will be provided for successful payments.

2. Requesting a Refund

Users may request support for issues such as duplicate payments, Premium not activating after a successful payment, service unavailability due to technical reasons, or unauthorized payments. Requests will be reviewed against transaction records.

3. Review and Resolution

Each request will be reviewed according to the actual payment status, service usage, applicable law, and payment-provider rules. Eligible refunds will be sent to the original payment method where possible. Banks or payment providers may require additional time to process the amount.

4. Cancellation

The cancellation method and its effect for any future Premium membership will be explained before checkout. Refund eligibility after digital access begins or content is used will be determined by applicable law and the terms displayed at checkout.

5. Safety When Requesting Support

Provide only the information needed for support, such as transaction ID, payment date, and amount. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password, or full card details.

6. Policy Changes

This policy will be updated to match the final payment process and applicable rules before payment features are launched.
